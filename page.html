    Season 1

    Kingpin race

    The kingdom with the most points is the Champion. For bowlers, after 16 weeks the one with the most pins is crowned Kingpin.

      How it works

      Rules of the clash

      • Each game: the team with more pins wins the game. A tie counts as half a game each.
      • Each night: six games. The team that wins more games wins the night.
      • Points: every game won is 1 point. Win 4 of the 6 games and you take 4 points, the other kingdom takes 2. A 3–3 night gives 3 points each.
      • Blind scores: some bowlers have a blind score. In the team total they count the blind score, or their real score when it is higher. Their own stats always show what they bowled.
      • Teams change: the players on Cholan and Pandiyan can change every week.
      • The season: 16 weeks. The kingdom with the most points at the end is the Champion. The bowler with the most pins is crowned Kingpin.

      Raavanaa League

      Rules of the realm

      Start on time, bowl in turn and let the screen settle the score.

      Before the first ball

      Time rules

      Starting time

      • Game starts at 10:00 AM sharp.
      • Lineups must be entered before practice begins.
      • Once practice time ends, the game must begin immediately, without wasting playing time.

      Late or not ready

      • If a player hasn’t arrived, their turn can be skipped up to 2 times.
      • If a player is not ready to bowl by their 3rd frame, they will:
        • Sit out the entire first game
        • Be awarded their average score (given at the time of team selection)
        • Still be charged for the game

      On the lanes

      Scoring rules

      1

      Strikes

      • If the machine shows a strike (displayed on the screen), it counts as a strike.
      2

      Spares

      • If the machine shows a spare (displayed on the screen), it counts as a spare.
      • If the machine accidentally clears all pins on the second ball (even if it was a split) but doesn’t register as a spare, it’s still a spare. It’s your lucky day!
      • If the machine accidentally removes a pin for the second ball, it’s also counted as a spare.
      3

      Wrong lane or wrong bowler

      What happens to a shot bowled on the wrong lane or out of turn.

      StrikeBecomes 9 and a spare

      X→9/

      SpareCancelled and reduced by 1 pin

      7/→72

      Open frameA missed spare or strike

      Stays as is
      4

      Missing or displaced pins

      • If a pin is missing on the first ball and the player bowls (accidentally or intentionally), the outcome stands as is. No re-throw allowed.
      • If a pin is in the gutter, it can be removed at any time. Players do not need to stop bowling, unless it distracts the next bowler.
      • If a pin is on the lane for the second shot, the bowler decides whether to keep it or have it removed.

      How the league was run

      League rules

      1. Admins are selected for every 16-week period.
      2. The same admin cannot be repeated in the same year.
      3. Everyone has to take a turn as admin. Anyone who doesn't want to be admin in a particular period doesn't get to vote on any deciding matters in that period.
      4. Admins can be selected in an order or by random draw among the possible players.
      5. Admins have veto power in that particular period.
      6. Admins can decide how to organize, pick teams, pick captains and set playing rules during that period.
      7. Any major decision about penalizing a player, or changing the playing time, date or venue, needs a 70% vote.

      Every Saturday

      The seven weekly categories

      Each category has a 1st, 2nd and 3rd place, ranked on that night's games.

      1. 1

        High Average

        Best average across the night's games.

      2. 2

        High Game

        Highest single-game score.

      3. 3

        Strike King

        Most strikes in the night.

      4. 4

        Spare Master

        Most spares in the night.

      5. 5

        Most 200s

        Most games of 200 or more. Only players who bowl a 200+ game can place.

      6. 6

        Clean Game

        A game with no open frames: every frame, the 10th included, is a strike or a spare. Ranked by how many clean games you bowl that night, not the score. Everyone with the most clean games takes gold, the next number down takes silver, then bronze. If nobody bowls one, no medal that week.

      7. 7

        Highest Low Game

        Take each bowler's worst game of the night. Whoever's worst game is the highest takes gold: the medal for staying steady all evening.

      Ties share the place. Because of ties, and weeks when nobody qualifies (for example in Clean Game), the number of medals each week can vary.

      End of the season

      The Season Medal Champion

      $300

      Most medals after 16 weeks

      The player with the most medals wins the special trophy and the $300 prize.

      • The champion receives the $300 prize only, without medal cash.
      • Every other player receives cash for all the medals they earned during the season.
      • If two players finish level on medals, the one with more gold wins. If still level, more silver.
      • There are up to 336 medal places in the season, including 112 golds. If every place is awarded, that is $196 in medal cash shared across the crew.
      • No minimum games. Miss a Saturday and you simply miss that week's chance to win medals.

      The MVP Award

      Each week, the team captains, the admin and a couple of core members jointly choose one MVP from across both teams.

      Weekly MVP

      Performance and sportsmanship come first.

      • Strong bowling
      • Contribution to the team
      • Fair play
      • Respect for others
      • Encouraging teammates

      MVP of the Season

      After 16 weeks, the player with the most weekly MVP awards is named MVP of the Season and receives a separate reward.

      A tie is decided by the same group, on overall performance and sportsmanship across the season.
